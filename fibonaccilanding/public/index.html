Frequently asked questions

How do I make a presentation from a topic?

Sign up, type a topic in one line, pick a design and start generation. In a minute you get ready slides with text and images. Nothing else to write.

Can I download the presentation as PowerPoint?

Yes. Two formats are available: .pptx for PowerPoint and PDF. The .pptx file opens and edits like a normal presentation — in Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides and Keynote.

Is it free?

Your first two presentations after signing up are free. After that there are paid plans starting at $1.99, plus a 14-day money-back guarantee. And each invited friend earns you a free presentation.

How long does it take to create a presentation?

About a minute from the moment you enter the topic. Usually the longest part is deciding on the topic itself, not waiting for the result.

What languages are presentations created in?

In more than 15 languages: English, Russian, Kazakh, Uzbek, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean and more. English is fully supported — from the structure to the captions on the slides.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. After generation the slides are edited right on the site — texts, headings and order. And after downloading as .pptx you can refine the presentation in PowerPoint like any ordinary file.

Does the service work in Telegram?

Yes, you can build a presentation right in the Telegram bot: send a topic — get a ready file in the chat. Handy when you're away from your computer.
